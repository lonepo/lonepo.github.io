01 — About

Who I Am

I'm an Electrical Engineering student who gets excited about connecting ideas that others think have nothing to do with each other — a power converter talking to a web dashboard, a Bluetooth signal guiding someone who can't see, an AI asking the right question at the right moment.

I run Linux, contribute to open-source, and believe the best software is the kind that gives people back their freedom. I tinker with hardware the same way — not to build impressive things, but to build useful ones.

I help people without expecting anything in return. That's just how I'm wired.
